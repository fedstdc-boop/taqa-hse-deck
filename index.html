Two core services: inspection and survey

Inspection

  • High-zoom visual inspection of insulators, cross-arms, pole-top hardware and conductors
  • Radiometric thermal imaging to locate hotspots, failing joints and overloaded components (Matrice 4T range: −20°C to 550°C*)
  • Flown from the ground, often with the line energised

Survey and mapping

  • LiDAR point clouds of poles, towers, conductors, corridors and vegetation
  • Photogrammetry for orthomosaics, 3D models and as-built records
  • Repeatable data capture without surveyors on foot in the corridor
*Matrice 4T temperature measurement: −20°C to 150°C in high-gain mode, 0°C to 550°C in low-gain mode.

Two-year contract with TAQA Distribution, signed in November 2023

Challenge

Inspect 17 overhead feeders on the 33 kV and 11 kV network, including long sections with no vehicle access.

Inspected all 19 feeder segments in eleven phases between February 2024 and December 2025, working to TAQA Distribution’s specifications and permit-to-work systems.

Deliverables

Monthly inspection reports against the agreed scope, with data collected for 309 km of circuit.

Sections with no vehicle access inspected without crews walking in or moving heavy plant.

Power line LiDAR survey: mobile mapping and drone

Right-of-way surveys of overhead lines across the UAE

Challenge

Build an accurate, georeferenced baseline of each corridor: towers, conductors, terrain, and the sand and vegetation encroaching on the lines.

What we did

Combined vehicle-mounted mobile mapping along access roads with drone LiDAR over towers and spans, processed through an automated, AI-assisted workflow.

Deliverables

Survey report, classified 3D point cloud, surface and terrain models, georeferenced 2D maps and GIS-linked tower data, with QA/QC reporting (RMSE, GCP checks, RTK/PPK logs).

Safety outcome

No surveyors walking the corridor or working beneath live spans.

Conductor sag and clearance analysis

Desert distribution corridor affected by dune encroachment

Challenge

Confirm that conductors keep a safe distance from ground and vegetation where shifting sand and growth reduce clearance over time.

What we did

Extracted conductors from the LiDAR point cloud and measured clearance span by span, then profiled sag, checked pole lean and verified pole-top components.

Deliverables

Clearance-violation report, sag profiles, sand and vegetation risk heatmaps with severity ranking, and a prioritised intervention list.

Safety outcome

Clearance risks ranked from the data before they become incidents, with no manual measurement at height.

Thermal inspection

Multi-year aerial inspection programmes for overhead transmission lines

Challenge

Find failing joints, clamps and insulators on energised lines before they fail, across thousands of towers.

What we did

Captured radiometric thermal, high-zoom RGB and UV corona imagery by drone, with AI models that detect tower components and flag anomalies.

Deliverables

Inspection report with a defect register, close-up images, temperature readings, severity ranking and verified technical recommendations.

Safety outcome

Defects found from outside minimum approach distances, with no climbing and the line in service.

Power line cleaning

Use case: live-line insulator washing, guided by IEEE standards for insulator cleaning

Challenge

Truck-based washing needs a supervisor and six tower men per team, uses up to 3,000 litres of water a day and exposes crews to electrocution and falls, often with a planned shutdown.

How it works

A drone-mounted cleaning payload washes insulators while the line stays energised, flown by a crew of one to three.

Outputs

Cleaning report with before-and-after imagery and a completion log.

Safety outcome

No tower men at height or near energised conductors, and no shutdown required.
